Our vision

A New Era in Agriculture

We believe the future of agriculture starts with the soil. We believe in an agricultural system where healthy, resilient soils help plants make better use of available water, withstand climate stress, and establish more reliably.

At AlgiSoil, we are building a bridge between nature, science, and everyday farming. We transform the potential of natural materials into practical, scalable soil technologies that can help farmers adapt to the challenges of the 21st century.

Our ambition goes beyond a single product. We want to build a new generation of soil solutions that make climate and drought resilience more accessible — from vineyards and orchards to farms across different regions and growing conditions.

Because the future of agriculture will not be built on using more. It will be built on using what we already have more efficiently and more consciously.

Products the soil needs. Solutions farmers can rely on. Built for drought. Built to scale. Built for the future.

Our story

AlgiSoil is, at its core, a story of friends.

AlgiSoil was born from the curiosity of friends and a shared desire to create something genuinely useful.

We came from different areas of agriculture, but we were seeing the same changes: drought, water scarcity, and declining soil health. Often over a glass of wine, we kept asking what we could build as a real response.

We wanted to develop a solution grounded in science, tested in real conditions, and genuinely useful for farmers.

Perhaps it is no coincidence that we are all Scouts: when we see a problem, we want to do something about it. That is how conversations became a shared mission — and that mission became AlgiSoil.

Starting with the soil, because that is where resilience begins.

Why alginite?

Nature's hidden soil regenerator

Alginite is a natural blend of fossilized algal biomass and mineral components. Its organic matter content, porous structure, and mineral composition make it a promising starting material for developing technologies that support soil water management and biological function.

Among the natural materials we know, alginite is the best fit for our innovation, our technology, and the goals we aim to achieve: improving soil water retention, supporting the root zone, and increasing plant resilience to drought.

We don't believe in promises — we believe in results. Because everything starts with the soil.
