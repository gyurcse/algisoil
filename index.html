Our vision

A New Era in Agriculture

Agriculture is changing, and the soil is often where that change becomes visible first. Longer dry periods, higher temperatures and less predictable rainfall are making water harder to keep where plants need it most. At the same time, many soils are losing structure, organic matter and biological activity — exactly the qualities that help them cope when conditions become difficult.

For growers, this is very practical. Young plants establish more slowly, roots face greater stress, irrigation becomes more important, and every failed planting has a cost.

We believe part of the answer is already beneath our feet. At AlgiSoil, we bring together farmers' experience and scientific research to understand what soils need under real growing conditions. We develop bio-based solutions that work in the root zone, helping soils retain water and giving young plants a better start when water is limited.

Because resilience is not an abstract idea. It is whether a plant establishes, whether a grower needs to irrigate again, and whether a field can remain productive through another dry season.

Products the soil needs. Solutions farmers can rely on.

Our story

AlgiSoil is, at its core, a story of friends.

We came to agriculture from different directions, but quickly found ourselves talking about the same things. Often over a glass of wine: what is changing around us, what growers are already dealing with, and whether we could build something genuinely useful in response.

Drought, water scarcity and declining soil health kept coming back into those conversations. Not as distant problems, but as things we were already seeing in the fields.

We were never interested in building something that worked only in theory. We wanted to create a practical solution — something rooted in science, tested in real conditions, and useful to the people working with the soil every day.

That is how AlgiSoil began.

Starting with the soil, because that is where resilience begins.

Why alginite?

Nature's hidden soil regenerator

For us, alginite is not just an ingredient — it's a starting point. A natural, versatile medium that restores soil structure, retains water, and brings life back into the system.

Alginite is one of nature's hidden reserves: a unique combination of fossilized algae and volcanic clay, forming a powerful soil-regenerating material. It improves structure, holds water, and reactivates biological life in the soil.
